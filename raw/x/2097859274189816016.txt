Apple just announced that Siri will be able to take actions inside third-party apps.

Talk to Siri, and it can take actions those apps make available through App Intents.

And since vibe coding mobile apps is solved (it is), you’ll be able to create your own apps and control them through Siri.

“Hey Codex, build me an app that tracks my workouts, finances, and meals. Make every action controllable through Siri via App Intents.”
