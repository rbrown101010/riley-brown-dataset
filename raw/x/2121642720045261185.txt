The Fable of Clawd

A 2 minute 4K film made entirely in Blender, from one prompt on Fable 5.2. It took about 4.5 hours.

Claude built every asset in Blender by writing Python:
- 5 characters and props: Clawd, his friend Pip, a toy truck, a 3D-printed tulip and a gift box
- 7 full sets: a study, a staircase, a street, a workshop, an airport, a night sky and a house
- every camera move and every frame of animation across 52 shots

That came to 12,500 lines of code. It started 7 copies of itself to build the sets at the same time.

Then it rented 89 GPUs through RunPod to render 3,220 frames as fast as possible, 36 of them running at once. Total cost: a little over $20.

My biggest takeaway is my Claude Code knows it has access to cloud compute, so it's much more ambitious.

I tried a similar prompt on my other computer that doesn't have the runpod skill setup and it wasn't nearly as high quality
